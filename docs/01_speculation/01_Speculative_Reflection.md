1. Title \- Revolt 86  
   Revolt 86 is a two player trading card war strategy board game that takes place in a dystopian future where war consumes the world. 

2. This will be a fabricated board game where electronics are used to immerse the participants and create a bridge between the game itself and reality. Each participant will use and create a deck of cards which will be used during the game. Each card will be categorized in one of three different types, and each card will have a different action. There are two players, two sides, one player will be the oppressor and the other will be the oppressed. The oppressed are always depicted by a blue color and they will always go first. Each player will take turns rolling a die and basing their actions on that dice roll. The goal is to destroy each other's home base, also known as the Mothership. Over time each participant will feel a sense of adrenaline and hyper focus while creating a strategy based on what cards they're given. Each participant will notice how the unpredictability of the game's mechanics can work in their favor or against them. 

3. Board games like chess have been around forever, but only just recently have they modernized the market with a smart chess board. A smart chess board can detect what piece is on the board and when that piece is about to move when a player touches it. The board then lights up potential movements on the board. They have made boards like these mainly for games that have been around and are known as classic board games. There are also smart boards that play many different simple fast games all in one board. But this concept of a smart board is more of a console. The Revolt 86 Game Board Console… A proprietary smart board that helps enhance the base game. 

4. This concept first started in my sophomore year of college. We were tasked to create a board game and at that point of time I was heavily playing chess. I love the endless outcomes that chess creates when being played. Chess is a simple game, as it only requires 32 game pieces with a clean checkered board. So I wanted to create a game that took these mechanics and enhance it with cards, dice, movesets, damage counters. Now we have created a vast amount of new outcomes… Now mix this with robots and war and we have a two player trading card futuristic war strategy game. This new game board “console” concept takes aspects of my board game and aspects of modern day consoles and combines them in one, a well balanced combination of physical board game mechanics and digital mechanics that work together to handle the game logic. A wired to the wall game board that connects to your wifi in order to connect with the Revolt 86 app on your phone and download possible updates when new cards or pieces release as they come with new game logic. Two smart health bars that physically connect to the board itself and to the Revolt 86 app on your phone using bluetooth to digitally handle player stats as well as connecting your personal Revolt 86 account. A board that tracks and reads every piece on the board. A board that reads the dice roll, and lights up the direction you can move or shoot in. A card reader that applies its effects of the card on the board and communicates with the Revolt 86 app on your phone.
