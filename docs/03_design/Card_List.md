1. H.E.A.T. Warhead (Avant) (WARSHIP)

Use once after rolling. Warship Ability.
Instead of moving your Warship, convert your entire die roll to launch this H.E.A.T Warhead at any enemy piece. The H.E.A.T. Warhead can only travel toward your opponent’s side of the board but can maneuver forward, left, to strike any enemy piece in range. 

2. Energy Rounds (Avant) (MORTAR)

Use once at the beginning of your turn. Mortar Ability.
Your mortar does +1 damage for a total of 2 damage. Discard after use. 


3. Illusive Force Field (Artifice)

Use once at the start of any turn. Concealed until triggered.
Select an open square on the board using an Artifice Tracker. Units cannot shoot or move through this square. Triggered when shot at or if an opponent attempts to enter this square. You may remove this Artifice card from play during your turn. 


4. Hyper Beam Cannon Attachment (Avant) (CANNON)

Use once at the beginning of your turn.
Add 2 to your roll. This cannon is unable to move this turn. Use the number you rolled to shoot at an enemy. This attack deals 1 damage to all enemies in the line of sight up to the rolled distance. 

5. System Overdrive (Avant)

Use once during your turn. 
Add 2 to your roll. Discard after use. 

6. System Hack (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
On your opponents next turn, subtract 2 from the number they roll.

7. Hard Reset (Avant)

Use once during your turn. 
Choose a friendly piece that is currently on your opponent's side of the board. Reset its position to its starting position on the board. 


8. Oblivion Artillery (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
You have activated your Mothership's Oblivion Artillery. This weapon deals its default amount of damage but gains the ability to shoot 6 spaces away in any direction.

9. Defensive Recall (Hyper) 

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Choose a friendly piece on the opponent’s side of the board. Reroll the die and move that piece backwards in any direction (including diagonally) up to the new number rolled. You cannot shoot. 

10. Mech Mirage (Artifice) (Mech)

Use once at the beginning of your turn. Remains concealed until triggered.
Using an Artifice Tracker, select a friendly Mech from anywhere on the board. As long as this Mech remains stationary and does not shoot, this Mech will remain immune to attacks. All units may move through this Mech. Triggered when shot at. If the Mech moves or shoots, this Artifice is destroyed and discarded. 

11. HyperShield (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Grants a temporary forcefield only to the war piece that got the elimination. If a war piece already has a HyperShield then this is rendered useless (HyperShields don’t stack). HyperShields add 1 HP on top of the piece's health but does not take away any damage counters (Doesn’t heal).


12. Last Resort (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Detonate the piece you activated this turn or the piece that secured the elimination draw. This will instantly eliminate your war piece along with your opponent's war pieces within a 3 x 3 grid radius centered on it. Mothership only takes 1 damage.


13. Power Cell (Avant)

 Remove 1 damage counter from any friendly piece on the board, including the Mothership (+1HP).



14. Rolling Thunder (Avant) (WARSHIP)

Use once after rolling. 
Move your warship the number of spaces you rolled without attacking. For every enemy war piece you fly over deal 1 Damage to them.

 
15. Anti-Artifice EMP (Avant)

Use once during your turn. 
Choose one Artifice that your opponent has in play. This Artifice card gets immediately returned to your opponent’s hand and any associated Artifice Trackers are wiped. 


16. Siege Mode Override (Artifice) (Mech)

Use once at the beginning of your turn. Do not conceal.
Using an Artifice Tracker, track a friendly Mech. This Mech can no longer move but its base attack damage is increased to 2 while in Siege Mode. If this Mech is moved or if this Mech is eliminated, destroy this Artifice and revert its damage. 

17. Repair Station (Artifice)

Use at the beginning of your turn. Do not conceal.
Track a vacant square on your side of the board using an Artifice Tracker. This places a 1HP Repair Station. For each elimination as long as this Repair Station remains intact, select a friendly piece with damage on it and heal 1 HP from it. Mothership is excluded. 

18. Core Siphon (Artifice)

Use at the start of your turn. Concealed. No Artifice Tracker Needed.
Keep this card face down. Anytime you score an elimination, reveal and discard this card to draw 1 extra card (2 cards total for that elimination). Core Siphon does not stack with other Core Siphon cards.

19. System Overload Virus (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Force your opponent to shuffle their entire current hand back into their deck, then draw cards equal to the number of cards they just shuffled in. Discard after use. 

20. Phase Shift (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Before your turn ends, roll again and only the piece that was just played gets to move left or right the number of spaces that was just rolled.

21. Artificial Worm Hole (Artifice)

Use at the beginning of your turn. Do not conceal.
Use 2 Artifice Trackers, track one vacant square on your side of the board and one vacant square on your opponent’s side of the board to establish a wormhole link. Any friendly or enemy piece that lands on either tracked square gets instantly transported to the linked square. You may remove and discard this card from play at any time during a turn.

22. Antimatter Trip Wire (Artifice)

Use at the beginning of your turn. Concealed until triggered.
Use 2 Artifice Trackers. Track one vacant square on the board. Roll the limitation die. Place your second tracker that many spaces to the left or to the right of the first tracker to form a horizontal line. The first enemy piece that enters or crosses any square along this horizontal line will trigger the Antimatter Trip Wire and explode, taking 2 damage. 

23. Vector Intrusion (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Without looking at your opponents cards, pick a card from their hand and discard it.

24. Defense System Override (Avant) (MOTHERSHIP)

Use once at the very start of your turn.
For this turn, you can only roll to shoot from your mothership. Discard after use.

25. ower Core Jumpstart (Avant)

Use once at the very start of your turn.
Roll the limitation die twice and use the greater roll to move or shoot.

26. Riot Mode Override (Artifice) (MORTAR)

 Use once at the beginning of your turn. Do not conceal.
Using an Artifice Tracker to track a friendly mortar. This mortar can no longer move but receives one less damage every time it's attacked. If this mortar is moved or if this mortar is eliminated destroy this artifice and revert its effects.

27. Dual Mecha Link (Avant) (MECH)

Use at the very start of your turn.
Roll your limitation die and distribute it between two different friendly Mechs. 

28. Overclock Gambit (Hyper)

Play immediately when drawn. Or at the end of your turn if in your starting hand.
Roll your limitation die. 
[4-6 SUCCESS] Your opponent skips their next turn.
[1-3 FAILURE] Discard a card from your hand. Then discard this card.
