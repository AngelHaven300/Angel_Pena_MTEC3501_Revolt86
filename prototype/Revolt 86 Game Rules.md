***Rules***

***Setting Up the Board***

Players will set up their pieces on the board, starting from the bottom left corner. The order of pieces are as follows; cannon, warship, mortar, mothership (takes up two squares), mortar, warship, and cannon ending on the bottom right corner. Then both players will line up their mechs in front of each piece that has been placed for a total of 16 pieces on one side of the board. 

First both players will shuffle their 24 card deck that can contain three types of cards, Avant cards, Artifice cards, and Hyper cards. And a max of two of the same card. They will place these cards to their right next to the game board. At the start of the game, players will draw 5 cards from the top of their deck. Afterwards players can only draw a card if they succeed at eliminating an enemy war piece. Players can only use one card every turn unless a card allows another card to be used.

The oppressors are always represented by the red pieces and the oppressed or the rebels are represented by the blue pieces. Blue will always go first to roll the die. 

***Turn Structure*** 

1. ***Roll \-*** Roll the limitation die.

2. ***Action \-*** Choose one of your pieces. Use the rolled number to split between Moving and Shooting. You may move first, then shoot or shoot first, then move or you could either just move or just shoot. All actions happen in strict real-time. Range and line-of-sight are always measured from the exact square your piece is physically standing on at the moment you declare the action.  
   This means:  
     
   If you **Shoot then Move**: You check line-of-sight and range from your *starting* position.  
   If you **Move then Shoot**: You check line-of-sight and range from your *ending* position.  
 


3. ***Card Phase \-***  You can play a maximum of one card from your hand every turn (Unless a card you’ve played says otherwise). Whether you play the card at the beginning of your turn or after you roll the die depends on the card’s conditions itself. 

4. ***Eliminations \-***  For each opponent’s war piece you have eliminated this turn, you draw a card.

5. ***End Turn \-***  Pass the turn to the opponent. 

***Rolling The Die***

When a player rolls the limitation die (movement and shooting die), the player picks only one piece on the board to move the rolled number based on the piece's unique movement set. If the player decides to shoot then they have to follow each piece's shooting limitations (whether they can shoot straight or diagonally). During movement, your pieces cannot move through enemies or teammates. During shooting, your pieces cannot shoot through teammates. 

Example: If you roll a 5 and you decide to move one of your Mechs 3 spaces on the board you would have 2 spaces left to shoot, so your maximum shooting range for this turn is 2 spaces. Because you moved a Mech you would only be allowed to move your piece forward, left, and or right by 3 spaces. And you could only shoot straight forward, to your left, and or to your right with a maximum of 2 spaces. 

***Types Of Cards***

There are 3 types of cards. (4 with next update)

Artifice Cards (Emerald Metallic) \- Artifice Cards can only be used in the beginning of your turn. When you draw an Artifice Card you must place it into your hand and cannot be used until your next turn. There can only be a max of 2 Artifice Cards active at a time during the game for each opponent. If the Artifice Card you played says you must conceal this card until the conditions have been met you will place the card face down to the left of the board otherwise place the card face up. These Artifices linger until the opponent triggers them or if the conditions on the card have been met. If a card tells you to select a square on the board use the Artifice Tracker to keep track of which square you selected. If the Artifice states it can be removed from play during a turn then doing so will prevent you from playing a card that turn. 

Avant Cards (Black Metallic) \-  Avant Cards can be used either in the beginning of your turn or at the end of your turn based on the card's conditions. When you draw an Avant Card you must place it into your hand and cannot be used until your next turn.

Hyper Cards (White Silver) \- Hyper Cards that are already in your opening hand can only be used at the end of your turn. The Hyper Cards that you draw after eliminations cannot be placed into your hand and can only be used immediately after drawing it. If you choose not to use it or it is unable to apply to your current turn, you must place it in your discard pile.

***Taking Damage*** 

Once a piece hits an opponent, based on the damage each piece does (1 damage or 2 damage) they will put a damage counter on the affected opponent. A piece is at full health when there are no damage counters underneath it. If an opponent's piece has 3HP and you do 1 damage to it, then you put a yellow damage counter underneath the opponent’s piece, if you do 1 more damage to it then you put a red damage counter which symbolizes it has 1HP left. Doing damage to a war piece that has 1HP leads to elimination and must get taken off the board. Mechs have a base of 2HP. If they take 1 damage, they get a red counter. If they take 2 damage they are eliminated. The mothership takes up 2 squares at the middle edge of the board, so each square has 3HP for a total of 6HP. Mothership health is kept track by its health bar attached to the board. For each damage received to the mothership the player removes a Power Cell from the health bar. 

**Mothership**

The Mothership is a stationary fortress that deals **2 damage** per shot and can fire straight, left, right, and diagonally. 

**Activating The Mothership:** The Mothership cannot be selected to shoot using standard die rolls alone. It can *only* fire if you play an Avant-card that explicitly powers up or triggers the Mothership's weapons during your Card Phase.

**Firing The Mothership:** When an Avant card allows the Mothership to fire, the player must choose only *one* of its two squares (Left or Right) to originate the attack. Range is calculated from that specific square using your remaining roll points, and a square cannot fire directly through its sister square.

***Upgrading Mechs*** 

If a mecha gets to the end of the board on your opponent's side, then it gets upgraded to either a cannon, a mortar, or a warship only if one of these pieces have been eliminated from your squadron. Remove all damage counters from the Mech as this upgraded piece spawns in with full health. 

***Winning The Game***

Once a mothership runs out of Power Cells, the game ends and the player whose mothership remains is declared the winner. If all pieces on the board (excluding both motherships) are dead then the game ends in a stalemate.
